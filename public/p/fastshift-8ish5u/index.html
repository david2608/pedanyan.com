What is checked

Checklist

  • UX best practicesUsability heuristics, task flow, clear next step on every screen.
  • Platform conventionsiOS Human Interface Guidelines and Android Material patterns.
  • Contrast ratioWCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text, icons and controls.
  • Text size and readabilityMinimum sizes, line length, support for larger system text.
  • Tap targetsAt least 44 × 44 pt (iOS) and 48 × 48 dp (Android), enough spacing.
  • Colour useStatus not shown by colour alone, consistent meaning of colours.
  • Visual hierarchyMain action and key information stand out first.
  • Screen statesEmpty, loading, error, success, offline.
  • Forms and validationLabels, keyboard types, input masks, error messages, limits.
  • Money clarityAmount, fee, total, recipient and status shown before and after payment.
  • Consent and legal textNo pre-ticked boxes, consents readable and in the user’s language.
  • Navigation and namingEntry points, back behaviour, the same names for the same things.
  • ConsistencyComponents, icons, spacing and wording used the same way across flows.
  • LocalisationArmenian, Russian and English text length, date and currency formats.
